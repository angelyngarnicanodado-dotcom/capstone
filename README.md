## Arduino Code additional Info
SmartHatch is designed to support small-scale poultry livelihoods in Siaton, Negros Oriental, by solving the risks associated with manual incubation and frequent power instability. Traditional methods in Barangay Mantuyop often result in hatch rates as low as 50% due to human error in egg rotation and climate management. This project integrates multiple sensors and IoT modules to automate the regulation of temperature, humidity, and mechanical egg turning. By utilizing a solar-powered backup system, SmartHatch ensures 24/7 operation during outages, while an ESP32-CAM and GSM module provide real-time visual streaming and SMS notifications to the farmer.
